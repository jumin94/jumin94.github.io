Hello! I am an Argentine climate scientist working on **regional climate variability and change**, mostly in the Southern Hemisphere. I use *storylines*, **causal inference** and statistical tools to work out what we can and cannot say about future climate, and to turn that into information people can use.

I trained as a geophysicist in La Plata and did my PhD in Atmospheric and Ocean Sciences at the University of Buenos Aires, co-advised from Reading. I am now a Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellow in Leipzig and PI of the Horizon Europe project FUTURA.
