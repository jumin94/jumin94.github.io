title: Emerging dynamical storylines can constrain near-term projections of the Southern Hemisphere eddy-driven jet

The eddy-driven jet steers the storms that shape weather across the Southern Hemisphere, but models disagree on how its drivers will change. I show how a causal framework that combines observations, reanalyses and dynamical storylines from CMIP projections can attribute past changes in the jet and narrow down its plausible near-term trajectories.
