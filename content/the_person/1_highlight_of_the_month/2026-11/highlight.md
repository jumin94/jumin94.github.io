With Marlene Kretschmer, we have a paper under review at BAMS on using **Bayes factors** to weigh competing scientific hypotheses in climate science, rather than relying only on p-values. Bayes factors provide a principled way to quantify the evidence for one hypothesis over another. This work offers practical guidance, illustrated with clear examples from climate science.
