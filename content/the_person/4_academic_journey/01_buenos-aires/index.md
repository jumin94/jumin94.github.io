*My childhood and teenage years were quite hippie, as my stepmom always likes to remind my brother and me. In Villa Elisa it was all about being outdoors, singing Brazilian songs, reading poems and listening to the birds sing. I could not ask for a better childhood. I have no doubt that it's the love for nature that I experienced very day living there what drives me and shapes the work I do.*
