title: Common sense, reduced to calculation
---
> “Probability theory is nothing but common sense reduced to calculation.” Pierre-Simon Laplace, *Philosophical Essay on Probabilities* (1814)

For me, Bayesian thinking is a daily workout for my brain. At the beginning it's hard (at least it was hard for me), but once you stick around long enough, it's the only way of thinking about probability that makes sense. First state what you believed before you collected your data, look at the evidence, and say how much it should change your mind. It is what we do (with simple problems) without even thinking about it. With Marlene Kretschmer we propose **Bayes factors** as a way to do exactly that when climate scientists weigh competing hypotheses. Writing this paper was a challenge: I struggled to find the right level of detail, the examples and the framing of the hypotheses. I really hope it's useful!
