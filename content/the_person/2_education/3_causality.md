title: From correlations to causes

link_text: Explaining and predicting the Southern Hemisphere eddy-driven jet (PNAS, 2025)
---
Climate science is full of correlations, but the questions people care about are causal: what drives the rain in my region, and what would change if those drivers changed? Looking for a language for those questions led me to causal inference, and to **Marlene Kretschmer**, with whom I now work in Leipzig. We use causal diagrams to write down our hypotheses and assumptions, so that others can see them, question them and test them. We then use them to guide our data analysis.
