*I read this while I was studying to write our paper on Bayes factors in climate science. It was the perfect motivation, because this book really helps you understand how easy it is to misinterpret results if we only use frequentist tools to quantify the evidence for them. It's also really fun to read, because it tells the story of statistics as we learn it at university.*
