BOOKS I RECOMMEND
- One folder per book; the number sets the order (01_, 02_...).
- index.md: title, author, year, isbn, link (+ link_text) and your experience / why you recommend it.
  index.es.md: the Spanish title and text.
- Cover: put cover.jpg in the folder, OR just write the isbn: line and the cover is
  downloaded automatically from Open Library the next time you run `python3 build.py`
  (it is then saved in the folder). The ISBN is the 13-digit number in most bookstore links.
