Over and over, I'm tricked into thinking that the work we do in science is (up to now; things are changing) mainly reflected in our papers. However, a friend recently reminded me that on a daily basis we shape the academic landscape with our presence, our way of thinking, our conversations and our questions. Without even knowing the impact it can have, we have the huge privilege of taking time every day to work on our sensitivity and our understanding of the world. This is the real daily work. It's mostly shared only with our friends in science, our community. We don't put it out there much.

This section just aims to make everything that's behind the CV equally visible. And in putting it together, I learned a lot about myself. Since one of my greatest joys is to see my friends, mentors and students grow, and I wish I could hear more about what happens behind the scenes, I'm putting myself out there, just in case anyone is interested in anything I have to share :)

I'm also a migrant from South America living in Germany, and I have moved around a lot in my life. In this section I'm sharing some of these experiences in the hope that some of these stories will be useful to someone else going through similar things.
