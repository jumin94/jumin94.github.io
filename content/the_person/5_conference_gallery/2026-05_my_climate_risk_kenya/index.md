The workshops examined topics including meaningful participation, trust, local knowledge and epistemic justice, contributing to the ongoing dialogue on how climate research can better support society through inclusive and interdisciplinary approaches. [Read the full report here](https://loom.ly/x2hVkE0).

Personally, it was amazing to talk to the women and men who make the work of ICPAC possible. They are incredibly advanced in terms of climate services. The technical quality, the level of co-production and the people-centered approaches to climate services are really cool. It was super inspiring.

It was also amazing to visit Naivasha and Crescent Island in Lake Naivasha, and to walk close to giraffes, zebras, vultures, wildebeests and warthogs! This is where *The Lion King* (1994; my first favorite movie of all time) found its inspiration: the Disney artists spent a long time at Hell's Gate National Park sketching the towering cliffs and Fischer's Tower to create the iconic Pride Rock and the gorge where the wildebeest stampede happens. It's also where *Out of Africa* was filmed.
